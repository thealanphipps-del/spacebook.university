## 🚨 1. Known Failure Modes & Systemic Vulnerabilities

Publishing systemic risks and technical limitations is the hallmark of genuine scientific inquiry. Spacebook.University acknowledges the following critical vulnerabilities:

### A. Sybil Attacks & Collusive Identity Clusters
* **Risk:** While device attestation and biometric passkeys raise the marginal cost of creating accounts, well-funded adversaries or hardware botnets could still manufacture synthetic identities to farm low-tier quadratic votes.
* **Mitigation Frontier:** Enforcing multi-domain competency proofs and the Invariant LII unbroken contribution lineage chain.

### B. Attention Manipulation & Attention Farming
* **Risk:** Participants might deploy automated scripts or passive physical carriers to satisfy spatial and beacon check-ins without genuine cognitive or community engagement.
* **Mitigation Frontier:** Proof-of-Attentive-Synthesis protocols, entropy verification, and peer-reviewed human validation checkpoints.

### C. Governance Capture & Elite Formation
* **Risk:** Early participants and core doctrine authors could form self-reinforcing oligarchies that monopolize Master Steward and Constitutional Guardian tiers.
* **Mitigation Frontier:** Strict exponential time-decay ($k \approx 0.028881/\text{month}$) requiring constant renewal, coupled with Citizens Assembly veto referendums.

### D. Edge Compute Heterogeneity & Byzantine Latency
* **Risk:** Consumer mobile and laptop NPUs operate across wildly fluctuating battery, thermal, and network conditions. High dropout rates could stall distributed matrix evaluations.
* **Mitigation Frontier:** Redundant batch slicing, balanced ternary error reconciliation, and speculative fallback routing.

### E. Graph Drift & Epistemic Corruption
* **Risk:** Over successive epochs, the Neo4j HomeTree and Valkey context cube could accumulate stale, orphaned, or doctrinally diverging nodes.
* **Mitigation Frontier:** Continuous background sentinel watchdogs, automated stub purges, and cryptographic lineage hashes.

## ❓ 2. The Great Unresolved Questions

1. **The Objective Contribution Boundary:** Can creative, philosophical, or governance labor ever be evaluated with pure mathematical objectivity without human political bias?

3. **The Sovereignty vs. Coordination Tension:** At what threshold does collective civilizational coordination begin to infringe upon individual cryptographic self-sovereignty?
