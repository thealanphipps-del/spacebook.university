## 🧭 Executive Mission

SPU-0006 establishes the multi-decadal scientific agenda for Spacebook.University. The roadmap is structured across the four academic divisions, translating theoretical questions into falsifiable experimental milestones, open research challenges, and empirical benchmarks.

Rather than presenting an immutable product roadmap, this document outlines an open research agenda inviting researchers, cryptographers, economists, and systems architects to design experiments, propose counter-theses, and submit peer-reviewed findings.

---

## 🔬 Division 1: Attention Science (SPU-D1)
### *Focus: Cognitive attention modeling, neuro-attention interfaces, attention allocation, and ethical stewardship.*

### Core Open Questions:
1. **Ethical Attention Measurement:** Can human attention be quantified, proved, and rewarded without inducing surveillance tracking, cognitive fatigue, or addictive behavioral loops?
2. **Coordination Without Manipulation:** How can thousands of individuals voluntarily converge their attention upon shared physical landmarks or civilizational goals without algorithmic recommendation exploitation or dark patterns?
3. **Curiosity vs. Imposed Engagement:** What physiological and cryptographic metrics distinguish deep cognitive learning and exploration from passive superficial scrolling?

### Milestone Roadmap:
* **Phase I (Foundations):** Client-side zero-knowledge proof generation for spatial landmark proximity (RFC-0025); verification without storing raw coordinates.
* **Phase II (Entropy Modeling):** Proof-of-Attentive-Synthesis protocols detecting automated bots, screen scrapers, and artificial click-farms through biological entropy signatures.
* **Phase III (Attention Manifolds):** Topological mapping of collective human interest across the 7 Observational Spectrums in Spacebook 5D.

## ⚡ Division 2: Distributed Intelligence (SPU-D2)
### *Focus: Collective edge intelligence, heterogeneous NPU meshes, and privacy-preserving federated AI.*

### Core Open Questions:
1. **Economically Useful Edge Inference:** Can consumer laptop and smartphone NPUs (XDNA2, Apple Neural Engine, Snapdragon NPU) reliably pool idle compute cycles to evaluate planetary-scale models at lower cost than centralized hyperscale datacenters?
2. **Federated vs. Centralized Performance:** Under what conditions can a decentralized network of heterogeneous edge nodes achieve parity with, or exceed, a centralized cluster on complex reasoning tasks?
3. **Compute Fraud & Byzantine Attestation:** What cryptographic proof-of-compute mechanisms prevent malicious nodes from spoofing matrix multiplication results or returning fabricated outputs?

### Milestone Roadmap:
* **Phase I (Edge Probing):** WebNN and WebGPU browser attestation for local inference capability (benchmarked on AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 and consumer nodes).
* **Phase II (Balanced Ternary Batching):** Slicing large inference tasks into balanced ternary $(-1, 0, +1)$ work packets to minimize bandwidth and enable rapid checksum verification.
* **Phase III (Decentralized RAG Mesh):** Cross-node retrieval augmented generation querying local edge memories via encrypted vector similarities.

## ⚖️ Division 3: Governance Science (SPU-D3)
### *Focus: Capture-resistant self-governance, non-transferable trust scoring, and anti-plutocratic mechanisms.*

### Core Open Questions:
1. **Resilience of Soulbound Trust (gATTN):** Can non-transferable, reputation-derived trust scores definitively resist capture, collusive voting rings, and under-the-table bribery markets?
2. **Objective Competency Measurement:** How can multi-domain competency proofs be evaluated across engineering, economics, and jurisprudence without creating self-appointing gatekeeper oligarchies?
3. **Optimal Temporal Decay Functions:** What mathematical decay parameters ($gATTN \cdot e^{-kt}$) maximize governance responsiveness while preserving stability and preventing institutional amnesia?

### Milestone Roadmap:
* **Phase I (Lineage Auditing):** Verification of the 7-node unbroken causal chain in Neo4j under Constitutional Invariant LII ($\text{Contributor} \longrightarrow \text{Event} \longrightarrow \text{Reputation} \longrightarrow \text{Competency} \longrightarrow \text{Trust} \longrightarrow \text{gATTN} \longrightarrow \text{Tier}$).
* **Phase II (Quadratic Attention Simulation):** Live testbed of Citizens Assembly quadratic voting ($\text{Votes} = \sqrt{\text{Balance}}$) with a strict 5% individual influence ceiling.
* **Phase III (Negative Veto Enactment):** Formal integration of the High Council of Stewards and Constitutional Court negative veto rights to halt invariant breaches.

## 🏛️ Division 4: Civilization Engineering (SPU-D4)
### *Focus: Long-term institutional memory, doctrine continuity, and cross-generational survival.*

### Core Open Questions:
1. **Doctrine Maintenance Across Generations:** How can foundational constitutions, operational runbooks, and ethical boundaries survive the turnover of original founders, core developers, and stewards without degrading into myth?
2. **Permanent Institutional Reflexes:** Can failure post-mortems (RCAs) and incident doctrines be compiled directly into immutable graph-level reflexes that make repeating diagnosed errors impossible?
3. **Planetary Federation Without Empire:** How can independent geographic communities and sovereign mesh networks federate their knowledge and liquidity without centralizing into an authoritarian core?

### Milestone Roadmap:
* **Phase I (Fail-Once Inscription):** Grounding all past diagnostic failures permanently into the Neo4j HomeTree and Valkey substrate.
* **Phase II (Federation Waypoint Treaties):** Bilateral and multilateral cryptographic treaties between regional nodes establishing mutual waypoint recognition.
* **Phase III (Multi-Century Archive):** Self-healing, air-gapped cold vaults and decentralized Merkle tree archives designed for century-scale persistence.
