## 🌌 1. Declaration of Purpose

Human civilization is experiencing an unprecedented crisis of coordination. While global digital networks have connected humanity with millisecond latency, the dominant architectures of the digital age have turned human attention into an extractive commodity. Under surveillance capitalism:
* Human attention is harvested without genuine consent.
* Social graphs are centralized and weaponized for engagement loops.
* Plutocratic systems equate financial wealth with societal authority.
* Institutional memory decays within single generations.

**Spacebook.University exists to explore whether an alternative is mathematically and socially viable.**

We are a public research institution dedicated to investigating whether human attention, distributed edge computation, knowledge preservation, and civilizational governance can unite into a coherent, participant-owned substrate—without surveillance, without coercion, and without plutocratic capture.

---

## 🧭 2. Core Thesis: The Attention Civilization Substrate

The system is not a game, a trading bot, or a social network. It is an exploration of a unified tripartite substrate:

```
            Human Attention Network
                     ↓
        Distributed Compute Network (Edge NPUs)
                     ↓
        Attention Economics Network (Pure Value)
                     ↓
             Civilizational Trust
```

When attention produces verifiable knowledge rather than passive consumption, computation moves to the edge rather than central silos, and governance emerges from demonstrated competence rather than capital accumulation, a self-sustaining civilizational engine becomes possible.

## 📜 3. Foundational Commitments

1. **Reality is Never Simulated:** All sensory events, coordinates, and physical encounters ground in the real physical world (Layer 0).
2. **The Invariant of Individual Attention (Invariant XLVII):** No observation without consent. No computation without permission. No economic coordination without transparency.
3. **The Anti-Plutocracy Invariant (Invariant L):** Economic Capital must never purchase Governance Authority ($\text{ATTN} \mathrel{\kern-0.2em\not\kern-0.3em\Longrightarrow} \text{gATTN}$).
4. **Epistemic Continuity (Invariant XXIX):** Institutional reasoning, failure post-mortems, and doctrine must be preserved immutably across generations.
