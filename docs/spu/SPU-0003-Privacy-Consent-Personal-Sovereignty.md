## 🛡️ Executive Principle

Large-scale attention networks, edge compute meshes, and economic coordination layers inevitably collapse without trust. Trust cannot be achieved by gathering more data; it can only be achieved through **cryptographic, revocable consent**.

Constitutional Invariant XLVII establishes:
> **"No observation may occur without consent. No computation may occur without permission. No economic coordination may occur without transparency. No user may lose ownership of their attention exhaust."**

---

## 🚦 The Three Consent Data Zones

All data generated by participant devices is classified into three strict, unbreachable zones:

| Zone | Category | Storage & Routing | Invariant Enforcement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 **Green Zone** | Public Knowledge & Proofs | Publicly verifiable ledger (Valkey / Neo4j / Base) | Merkle proofs, RFC authorship, verified attestation proofs |
| 🟡 **Yellow Zone** | Ephemeral Operational Context | Local device cache / edge peer memory with microsecond TTL | Traversal metrics, network pings, temporary routing hops; auto-purged |
| 🔴 **Red Zone** | Raw Sensory & Private Data | Strict local-only hardware enclave (device storage) | Raw GPS breadcrumbs, biometrics, private keystores; **NEVER leaves device** |

---

## 🔑 The Four Consent Domains

Every participant explicitly toggles and authorizes four independent domains:

1. **Spatial Attention Consent:** Authorization to verify physical landmarks or location-aware beacons without tracking continuous transit paths.
2. **Compute Contribution Consent:** Authorization to utilize local NPU/GPU idle cycles for distributed inference batches.
3. **Epistemic Contribution Consent:** Authorization to anchor discoveries, research notes, and peer reviews to the public graph.
4. **Governance Engagement Consent:** Authorization to bind identity credentials to non-transferable gATTN voting records.

## 📲 Cryptographic Device Attestation

Device trust is established via asymmetric hardware-enclave attestations:
- **Non-Identifying Fingerprint:** Salted hash of hardware architecture and NPU availability.
- **Passkey Biometrics:** FIDO2 / WebAuthn client-side signatures.
- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs:** Proof of location proximity without revealing absolute coordinates.
