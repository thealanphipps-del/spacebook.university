The Statutory Commercial Matrix & Article 9 Scope

Module 2 • 3 Lectures Creation
The Sacred Triad of Attachment (§ 9-203)
The private contract creating an enforceable lien between debtor and creditor. Deconstructing the three non-negotiable statutory requirements of § 9-203(b).
  • Element 1: Value given by secured party (§ 9-203(b)(1))
  • Element 2: Debtor rights in the collateral or power to transfer (§ 9-203(b)(2))
  • Element 3: Authenticated Security Agreement with granting clause (§ 9-203(b)(3)(A))
  • Attachment timing, future advances, and after-acquired property floating liens (§ 9-204)
Module 3 • 4 Lectures Specificity
Collateral Classification & Anti-Supergeneric Traps
Statutory definitions of tangible, quasi-tangible, and intangible property under § 9-102. Understanding why supergeneric descriptions are valid for Form UCC-1 but void for Security Agreements.
  • Accounts, chattel paper, instruments, general intangibles, deposit accounts
  • Commercial tort claims: Strict specific identification rule (§ 9-108(e)(1))
  • Consumer goods restrictions under § 9-108(e)(2)
  • The fatal § 9-108(c) trap: Why "all assets" in a Security Agreement is completely void
Module 4 • 4 Lectures Perfection
Perfection Mechanics & Multi-Lane Notice (§ 9-310)
Constructive notice to the world. Examining the 5 statutory methods of perfection: filing, possession, control, automatic perfection, and notation on certificates of title.
  • Form UCC-1 filing requirements and Secretary of State registry rules (§ 9-501)
  • Perfection by control: Deposit Account Control Agreements (DACA § 9-104)
  • Control of investment property (§ 9-106) and electronic money (§ 9-105A)
  • Automatic perfection upon attachment: Consumer PMSI (§ 9-309(1))
Module 5 • 5 Lectures Priority
Priority Battles & The PMSI Superpriority (§ 9-322 / § 9-324)
The core economic battleground: Creditor vs Creditor. Applying the foundational "First to File or Perfect" rule and mastering the statutory exceptions that leapfrog prior blanket liens.
  • The general rule: First in time, first in right (§ 9-322(a)(1))
  • PMSI in Equipment: The 20-calendar-day possession grace period (§ 9-324(a))
  • PMSI in Inventory: Prior written notice requirement before delivery (§ 9-324(b))
  • Control priority over filing priority for deposit accounts (§ 9-327)
Module 6 • 3 Lectures Jurisdiction
Debtor Location (§ 9-307) & MTSTA Sovereign Compacts
Conflict of laws in multi-state commercial operations. Where to file? Analyzing the registered organization rule and sovereign cross-border tribal commercial transactions.
  • The Debtor Location Rule: State of formation for registered entities (§ 9-307(e))
  • Individual debtors: Principal residence vs location of assets (§ 9-307(b))
  • Post-closing debtor relocations and the 4-month reperfection rule (§ 9-316)
  • Model Tribal Secured Transactions Act (MTSTA Title 9) sovereign compacts
Module 7 • 3 Lectures Enforcement
Default, Repossession & Commercial Reasonableness
Remedies upon debtor default under Part 6 of Article 9. Self-help repossession, breach of the peace limitations, and strict requirements for commercially reasonable disposition.

Lecture 1: The Commercial Matrix & Article 9 Architecture

📚 UCC 101: Statutory Commercial Law Fundamentals

The essential 6-pillar conceptual matrix of UCC Article 9. Review before drafting any security agreement or financing statement.

🔗 1. The Holy Dual: Attachment vs. Perfection
Attachment (§ 9-203): Creates the enforceable lien between Debtor and Creditor. Requires (1) Value given, (2) Debtor rights in asset, (3) Authenticated Security Agreement with granting clause.

Perfection (§ 9-310): Gives constructive notice to the universe by filing Form UCC-1 with the central Secretary of State or obtaining control.
📜 2. Contract vs. Notice
The Security Agreement is the private substantive contract granting the lien. The Form UCC-1 is merely an administrative billboard notifying subsequent creditors of your claim. A creditor cannot file a valid UCC-1 without authenticated debtor authorization (§ 9-509)!
⚠️ 3. Debtor Name & SSL Trap (§ 9-503)
For registered entities, you must use the exact legal name in the public organic record (Articles of Incorporation). Under § 9-506(c), if the state's Standard Search Logic does not retrieve your filing due to a typo or trade name, your lien is seriously misleading and completely void!
🛡️ 4. Collateral Specificity (§ 9-108)
In the Security Agreement, descriptions must identify collateral by statutory category (accounts, equipment, inventory). Saying "all assets" in a Security Agreement is void under § 9-108(c), even though § 9-504 permits broad "all assets" on the UCC-1 notice!
🏆 5. Priority & PMSI Leapfrog (§ 9-324)
The general baseline is First to File or Perfect (§ 9-322). But a Purchase-Money Security Interest (PMSI) in equipment grants a 20-day grace period from debtor possession to file and leapfrog over prior blanket lienholders!
🌐 6. Debtor Location Rule (§ 9-307)
Where do you file? File where the debtor is located, not where the asset sits! Registered organizations reside in their State of Formation (e.g. Delaware, Nevada, Wyoming). Cross-border tribal transactions anchor to the Model Tribal Secured Transactions Act (MTSTA).
