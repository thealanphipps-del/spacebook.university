SPU-0001: The Spacebook Manifesto

DOCUMENT ID: SPU-0001 | CATEGORY: FOUNDATION | REVISION: 1.0

1. Declaration of Purpose

Spacebook.University is an open, non-hierarchical research institution dedicated to investigating whether human attention, distributed edge computation, knowledge preservation, and civilizational governance can unite into a coherent, participant-owned substrate—without surveillance capitalism and without plutocratic capture.

2. The Core Thesis

Traditional networks commodify user attention into behavioral ad profiles. Spacebook transforms attention into verifiable knowledge production, moving computation to the edge rather than central silos, and deriving governance authority strictly from demonstrated competency rather than capital accumulation.

3. Contributor Attraction Message

"Help us explore whether a civilization-scale attention, governance, and knowledge network can be built without surveillance capitalism and without plutocratic governance."
